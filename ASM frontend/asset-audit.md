# Official ASM CSIT Asset Audit

The authorized logo downloaded from the official ASM CSIT site is a combined 668×321 branding lockup. It includes the ASM CSIT mark, a B+ NAAC emblem, and “Approved by AICTE” wording on a transparent background. It must replace the invented `ASM` box mark and the generated compass mark in the header, footer, and chatbot header.

The official home banner is a 1920×350 wide image. It has a blue-to-green treatment with a group of formal student portraits on the right and an intentionally open colored field on the left. The homepage hero should reproduce this structure: full-width banner, responsive short height, left-aligned light textual hierarchy, and the actual image used as its visual base.

The remaining official banners will be used as site-section backgrounds or imagery instead of generated concept artwork. Generated Campus Ledger assets are now prohibited from the rebuilt visual design.

The official Why ASM banner is a 1920×750 deep navy photograph treatment with graduation caps on the right and deliberate dark negative space on the left. It supports an ASM-style full-width institutional section with white copy and left-aligned content.

The official Programs banner is a 1920×900 composition with blue-tinted graduates and caps in a narrow visual zone on the left while the large right area remains white. It is suitable for a two-column programme section with the real banner as the left image field and official-course content on the right.

The official homepage stylesheet identifies Montserrat as the heading family and Roboto as the primary body family. It uses a dark institutional navy near `#1c2d59`, bright blue link/highlight treatment near `#0095eb`, and a desktop logo/header height near 72px. These values should replace the previous invented editorial typography and palette.

The first two May 2024 website-gallery files are small 296×148 student-focused photographs. They are usable as official website assets for supporting content only, not as full-width hero material. The official wide banners remain the preferred visual base for the recreated hero and major content areas.
